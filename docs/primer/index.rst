Primer
======

This Primer explains the basics of Beneficial Ownership and the basics of a data standard handling Beneficial Ownership.

People new to the concept of :doc:`Beneficial Ownership <whatisbo>` will want to know what it is. Others will need to understand what the :doc:`data standard <whatisbods>` is and the :doc:`concepts <concepts>` that underpin it. Technology teams will be interested in the :doc:`data model <datamodel>` behind the standard.
